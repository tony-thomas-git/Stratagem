# Glossary

> **Summary.** Domain vocabulary for THIS project. Add a term here
> the first time it's used in a wiki page; subsequent pages link
> here for the definition. Distinct from `scopes.md` — that file
> defines TAG vocabulary; this file defines CONCEPT vocabulary.

---

## How to use

- When you write a term that future agents/readers might not know,
  add it here AS YOU WRITE IT. Don't batch.
- Cite the source. Most definitions trace back to vendor docs,
  internal memos, or industry standards.
- Link the wiki page where the term is most thoroughly explored, if
  applicable.
- Alphabetical order. Keep entries one paragraph max.

## What belongs here vs elsewhere

- **Belongs in glossary**: project-specific terms, acronyms,
  product/feature codenames, vendor-specific concepts your project
  has adopted.
- **Does NOT belong in glossary**: general industry terms
  (REST, SQL, gRPC, etc. — assume the reader knows these or can
  Google them). Detailed explanations of concepts — those go in
  their own wiki pages and the glossary just links to them.
